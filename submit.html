Before You Submit

Preparation checklist

  • Manuscript is anonymised for double-blind review
  • Abstract is 250–350 words
  • All figures and tables are included
  • References follow APA 7th or Chicago author-date
  • Declarations of interest are included in the cover letter
  • File is PDF or DOCX, under 25 MB

Review Process

  • Double-anonymized peer review
  • Initial editorial decision: 5–10 business days
  • Full review: typically 6–10 weeks
  • Authors are notified at every stage

Open Access

All accepted articles are published open access at no charge to authors at launch. No article processing charges (APCs) apply for this inaugural period.
